Visual comparison

Elapsed time: lower is only nominally better here. Each workload is designed to run for 30 seconds, so values near 30000 ms primarily show completion of the requested interval, not throughput.

Elapsed leader: ORCHESTRA is fastest at 4 workers (29412 ms), while CFS is fastest at 1 and 2 workers by small margins. This does not establish a throughput winner.

Context-switch leader: ORCHESTRA is lowest at 1 and 2 workers. At 4 workers, scx_simple is lowest (48408), ORCHESTRA is next (54126), and CFS is highest (96670). Lower context switching is a secondary overhead signal, not a complete fairness or latency metric.

Conclusion

The fixed VM comparison shows no new attach, progress, or unload failure after remediation. There is no single overall performance winner: ORCHESTRA leads the context-switch signal at 1/2 workers and the nominal elapsed result at 4 workers; scx_simple leads context switches at 4 workers; CFS has the lowest nominal elapsed time at 1/2 workers. Single-run VM measurements are not sufficient to attribute differences to a code fix. Repeated runs and physical hardware remain required for performance claims.
